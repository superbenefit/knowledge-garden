OpenCivics is a network of civic innovators, organizers, and patrons in service of a vital, resilient, and participatory civilization.

**If you align with this mission, you can choose a membership role to participate in the network, consortium, or labs.**

Learn more the process by reading about [Membership Roles](./Membership%20Roles.md), Membership Criteria, and [Application Guide](./Application%20Guide.md) before you apply for membership – then fill out the [Membership Application](http://go.opencivics.co/application) to choose how you’d like to participate.

# Ways to participate

Membership in the network is offered as various roles across the organizing structures of the Network.

#### Network Membership

- [Become an Ally](./Membership%20Roles.md#^e62138) : follow and contribute towards network activity
- [Become a Patron](./Membership%20Roles.md#^e62138) : resource network activity

#### Consortium Membership

- [Become a Contributor](./Membership%20Roles.md#^e62138) : contribute to a consortium initiative
- [Become a Citizen](./Membership%20Roles.md#Consortium%20Membership) : contribute to network governance
- [Become a Delegate](./Membership%20Roles.md#Consortium%20Membership) : speak on behalf of consortium members
- [Become a Steward](./Membership%20Roles.md#Consortium%20Membership) : responsible for maintaining network functions
- [Become an Advisor](./Membership%20Roles.md#Consortium%20Membership) : support network stewardship and governance

#### Labs Membership

*Members of the Consortium are invited to become Labs members*

- [Become a Participant](./Membership%20Roles.md#Labs%20Membership) : contribute to a labs project
- [Become a Practitioner](./Membership%20Roles.md#Labs%20Membership) : ...
- [Become a Partner](./Membership%20Roles.md#Labs%20Membership) : ...
- [Become a Manager](./Membership%20Roles.md#Labs%20Membership) : govern and operate the labs
- [Become an Operator](./Membership%20Roles.md#Labs%20Membership) : support day-to-day operations
